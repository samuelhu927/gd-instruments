Understanding the differences between spring types is crucial. Compression Springs are open-coil helical springs that offer resistance to compressive loading. Extension Springs are close-coiled helical springs that offer resistance to a pulling force. Torsion Springs exert pressure along a circular arc, providing torque. Each type requires specific testing methods and equipment.
