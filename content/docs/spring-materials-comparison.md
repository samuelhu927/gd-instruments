---
title: "Spring Materials: 65Mn vs 50CrVA vs Music Wire"
date: 2026-06-03
layout: "single"
---
Choosing the right material is critical. Music Wire (ASTM A228) is suitable for small, highly stressed springs. 65Mn (Manganese carbon steel) offers good hardenability but is prone to cracking. 50CrVA (Chrome-vanadium) provides excellent fatigue and impact resistance, matching SAE 6150, making it ideal for automotive and heavy machinery springs.
