date: 2026-06-01

---
Springs have a fascinating history dating back centuries. The concept of elastic energy storage dates back to ancient times with the bow and arrow. The modern coil spring was invented in the 15th century and used in locks. With the Industrial Revolution, the steel coil spring was first patented in America in 1857 for use in chair seats. In 1676, British physicist Robert Hooke postulated Hooke's law, establishing that the force a spring exerts is proportional to its extension.
