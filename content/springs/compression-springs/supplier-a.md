### About Us
Guangdong Spring Factory A is a leading manufacturer of heavy-duty springs. With over 15 years of experience, we supply high-quality springs to OEMs globally. Our factory is equipped with advanced CNC spring machines and automated heat treatment lines.

### Our Capabilities
- **Wire Diameter:** 3.0 mm to 50.0 mm
- **Material:** 50CrVA, 65Mn, Stainless Steel 304/316
- **Surface Treatment:** Zinc plating, Powder coating, Shot peening
- **Testing Equipment:** Fatigue testing machines, Hardness testers, **GD-S100 Spring Tension/Compression Tester**
